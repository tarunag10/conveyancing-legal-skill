## Status meanings

- **Outstanding:** not yet answered or evidence not yet received.
- **Evidence received:** material is present but has not been checked against the exact condition.
- **Verified:** authorised conveyancer has checked the evidence against the exact live condition; record reviewer and date.
- **Waived by lender:** written waiver/approval is held and its scope checked; cite the lender communication.
- **Not applicable:** reason recorded and confirmed by the authorised conveyancer; obtain lender confirmation where required.
- **Rejected / disputed:** escalate and record the lender response and next action.

Do not mark a condition verified because a document was merely uploaded or a keyword extractor found matching text. A lender waiver is not inferred from silence. This checklist does not itself determine whether a matter may proceed.
