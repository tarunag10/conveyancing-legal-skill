## Identify source, form and submission

- [ ] Read current lender instruction letter and all offer special conditions, including drawdown/notice.
- [ ] Check current England/Wales UK Finance Part 1 and applicable Part 2, clauses **4.5b**, **10.2b** and **1.11**; Part 3 where lender-only instructions are incorporated; note not applicable if non-participant/bespoke.
- [ ] Confirm product is residential / BTL / bridging / commercial / company / second charge / further advance / remortgage, and check any additional or third-party security.
- [ ] Confirm exact document: standard CoT / lender CoT / case eCoT / RoT by lender lawyer / separate-reporting document / other.
- [ ] Locate current form at official lender page or live case LMS/Lender Exchange/Smoove/other portal; confirm form version, jurisdiction and brand.
- [ ] Establish correct accepted submission method and case link, **not only LMS panel membership or public form presence**.
- [ ] Check LMS/electronic eligibility and firm panel membership; firm SRA/CLC details, nominated office and secured bank details.
- [ ] Extract official contact for legal issue, portal support, outstanding offer conditions and urgent completion.
- [ ] Verify lender notice **unit and start event**: ____ business/working/calendar days / hours; exact source and date: ____.
- [ ] Confirm completion date, offer expiry, bank cut-offs, advances, fees, bank verification and return-of-funds rule.

## Legal readiness and conditions

- [ ] Title, plans, restrictions, conveyancing searches and enquiries reviewed against lender requirements.
- [ ] Existing charge redemption/consent/priority and any second charge/additional security addressed with lender approval where required.
- [ ] Occupiers, tenancies, building safety, HMO, insurance, indemnities and search alternatives approved where necessary.
- [ ] Borrower and company powers, KYC, AML/SOF, gift, guarantee and required ILA/witness evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Pre-completion searches, updated offer, mortgage deed/ROT, undertakings and conditions documented.
- [ ] Material adverse issues separately reported; written lender acceptance/waiver on file. No invented approvals.
- [ ] Only authorised solicitor confirms unqualified CoT or reports qualifications and awaits written authority.
