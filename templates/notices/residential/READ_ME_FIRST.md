# Residential notices: England private sector post-1-May-2026

Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/assured-tenancy-forms , updated 1 May 2026; SI 2026/354 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2026/354/made . Do **not** create serviceable substitute Form 3A or Form 4A by copying this pack's worksheets. Form **3A is version-controlled by ongoing ministerial online publication**; always obtain the currently published official PDF and use the current guidance / legal wording for grounds. Form **4A** is the prescribed rent-increase notice; verify latest government form, notice period, effective date, rent periods and applicable transitional rules.

Separate from: social-housing tenancies, historic England Section 21/6A, notices served pre-1 May 2026, Wales Renting Homes occupation contracts, agricultural occupancies, licence or lodger arrangements. Never assume the same procedure across these categories. Part VI LTA 1987 sections 47 and 48 address notices are not the same as LTA 1954 business tenancy exclusion.

The two official-form worksheets, section 48 neutral address notice and section 47 information checklist are editable internal documents; only the legally appropriate instruments should be served. All dates and statutory conditions require live checking.
