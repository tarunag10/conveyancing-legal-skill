# Notice, search and leasehold precedent library — 2026-11-24

## Statutory prescribed forms (do not reconstruct from worksheets)
* `business/OFFICIAL_FORMS_1_TO_17.json`: all **17** LTA 1954 Part II Forms 1–17 under SI 2004/1005, with official form links and **17 internal preparation worksheets**. The worksheets are **not** forms for service.
* `../../lta1954/SURRENDER_SCHEDULE_3_WARNING_NOTICE_BLANK.docx`, `SURRENDER_SCHEDULE_4_P6_SIMPLE_DECLARATION_BLANK.docx`, `SURRENDER_SCHEDULE_4_P7_STATUTORY_DECLARATION_BLANK.docx`: special written-agreement-to-surrender route; distinct from Schedules 1 and 2 of the 2003 Order for new-grant contracting out.
* `residential/FORM_3A_POSSESSION_DRAFTING_WORKSHEET.docx` and `FORM_4A_RENT_INCREASE_DRAFTING_WORKSHEET.docx`: **not** serviceable Form 3A/4A; use current originals from https://www.gov.uk/guidance/assured-tenancy-forms .

## Solicitor-drafted, non-prescribed precedents
* `enforcement/`: two section 27 tenant notice models, section 29B extension agreement, contractual break, rent-review initiation, notice-to-complete, breach letter, section 146 **preflight worksheet only**.
* `leasehold/`: notice of transfer, notice of charge, request for licences to assign/alter, compliance certificate request, landlord/agent notices enquiry.
* `residential/`: section 48 service address notice and section 47 demand-details checklist where applicable.
* `../searches/`: **14** search order and result-review worksheets plus official HMLR and provider routing catalogue.
* `shared/`: service record and deadline tracker.

**Key rule:** no document is signed, served, sworn, lodged or certified by existence in this library. Real client facts stay in private matter storage. For any prescribed text, recheck current SI and official form version at point of use. For commercial lease renewals validate court/tribunal deadlines (s29A/s29B), service and rights of occupation. Never infer effective service from a draft.
