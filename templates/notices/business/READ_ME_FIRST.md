# 1954 Act business tenancy prescribed notices, Forms 1–17

**Authoritative sources**: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2004/1005/schedule/2/made and the government guidance (updated 30 July 2026): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/renewing-and-ending-business-leases-a-guide-for-tenants-and-landlords/forms-prescribed-under-the-landlord-and-tenant-act-1954-part-2-notices-regulations-2004.

The seventeen named forms are prescribed by SI 2004/1005, Schedule 2. **Do not serve** the accompanying `FORM_XX_PREPARATION_WORKSHEET.docx` as a section 25, 26 or 40 notice. Each worksheet is an internal matter-data and preflight checklist, deliberately **not a reconstructed statutory notice**. For each actual notice obtain and complete the authentic prescribed text from the live official SI or current government PDF/Word source and verify its edition, footnotes, conditions and jurisdiction before service.

Forms 7–17 concern particular statutory circumstances (e.g., Leasehold Reform Act 1967, special section 57/58 certificates, historical Welsh Development Agency categories). Those forms are **not interchangeable** with standard Forms 1–6. For qualifying pre-December-2017 telecom Code-rights tenancies check the government's **30 July 2026 tribunal-jurisdiction guidance** and case court/tribunal allocation.

Non-prescribed s27 tenant notices and s29B extension agreement are separately drafted editable precedents under `templates/notices/enforcement/`. New-tenancy contracting out uses **2003 Order Schedules 1–2**, whilst contractual surrender arrangements use **Schedules 3–4**. Do not confuse them.

Checked 24 November 2026. Live official form and law verification at every use remains mandatory.
