# Agreement to surrender a protected business tenancy (not a new lease)

**Must use separate 2003 Order Schedule 3 warning notice and Schedule 4 paragraph 6 simple declaration or paragraph 7 statutory declaration**, depending on whether the notice was served at least 14 days before *the first legally binding commitment*. Do not use Schedule 1 new-tenancy warning. Sources: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/schedule/3/made and https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/schedule/4/made .

Workflow: obtain existing lease/title and evidence of current statutory protection; ascertain the date and legal basis of agreement to surrender versus actual deed of surrender; identify landlord and tenant and authority; serve Schedule 3; count actual period to earliest binding agreement; make corresponding paragraph 6 or 7 declaration; include reference to notice and declaration in instrument creating the section 38A(2) agreement (Schedule 4 para 5); prepare a separate agreed surrender instrument if legally required; address rent/deposit/release/apportionments, third-party consents, guarantor, registration cancellation, occupation and keys. Secure human approval before service or signing. A written surrender warning/declaration does not itself surrender a lease. Do not represent a completed event without actual source evidence.

The bundled declaration precedents contain the official alternate wordings **unselected**. Remove inapplicable singular/plural or authority alternatives for an actual execution copy. No client details or fictitious dates may be stored in the plugin.
