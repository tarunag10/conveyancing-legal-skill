# Model wording to adapt in an EXISTING lease (not a substitute for statutory compliance)

**Use only for a new tenancy, subject to solicitor review, if contracting out is expressly agreed.** Where the existing lease has a clause 48 or other equivalent, preserve its numbering and structure. The `____` blanks below are only completed from proof of service and declaration. If the correct procedure has not occurred, do not represent these statements as true.

## Alternative A: Schedule 2 paragraph 7 (simple declaration; 14-day route)

**Exclusion of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954**

1. The Tenant confirms that, before entering into this Lease or becoming contractually bound to do so:

   (a) the Landlord served on the Tenant on __________ a notice in the form, or substantially in the form, set out in Schedule 1 to the Regulatory Reform (Business Tenancies) (England and Wales) Order 2003, at least 14 days before the Tenant entered into this Lease or became contractually bound to do so; and

   (b) the Tenant (or a duly authorised person on the Tenant's behalf) made a declaration on __________ in the form, or substantially in the form, set out in paragraph 7 of Schedule 2 to that Order.

2. The Landlord and Tenant agree that sections 24 to 28 (inclusive) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 shall be excluded from applying to the tenancy created by this Lease.

## Alternative B: Schedule 2 paragraph 8 (statutory declaration; short-notice route)

**Exclusion of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954**

1. The Tenant confirms that, before entering into this Lease or becoming contractually bound to do so:

   (a) the Landlord served on the Tenant on __________ a notice in the form, or substantially in the form, set out in Schedule 1 to the Regulatory Reform (Business Tenancies) (England and Wales) Order 2003; and

   (b) the Tenant (or a duly authorised person on the Tenant's behalf) made a statutory declaration on __________ in the form, or substantially in the form, set out in paragraph 8 of Schedule 2 to that Order.

2. The Landlord and Tenant agree that sections 24 to 28 (inclusive) of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 shall be excluded from applying to the tenancy created by this Lease.

**Review point:** Check the actual earliest binding event (including an agreement for lease), party identities, declaration type/date, and precise references required by Schedule 2 paragraphs 5 and 6. Do not copy the model if it creates an inconsistency with an existing instrument or if notice/declaration evidence is missing. This model is **not itself a prescribed form**; only Schedule 1 notice and Schedule 2 declarations are prescribed forms.
