# LTA 1954 contracting-out templates: usage map

**All templates are neutral and blank.** These are reusable Word precedents, not an executed or served document, and contain no client or case identifiers. Source: SI 2003/3096, official Schedules 1-2 (verified 24 November 2026): https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/pdfs/uksi_20033096_en.pdf . Re-verify at each use.

| File | Legal trigger | Only fill using evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `NOTICE_SCHEDULE_1_BLANK.docx` | **Every** valid new-lease exclusion path | Tenant and landlord full names and their addresses under To/From; nothing else in statutory body |
| `SIMPLE_DECLARATION_SCHEDULE_2_P7_BLANK.docx` | Warning notice served **at least 14 days** before commitment | Declarant, address, identity of tenant, property, term commencement, landlord, actual declaration date; choose prescribed alternatives; notice To/From; sign |
| `STATUTORY_DECLARATION_SCHEDULE_2_P8_BLANK.docx` | Warning notice served **fewer than 14 days** before commitment | Same identities, actual jurat date/place and oath-taker; choose prescribed alternatives; note statutory solemn declaration; notice To/From |
| `LEASE_CONTRACTING_OUT_CLAUSE.md` | Instrument creating the tenancy | Actual verified service date, declaration route, declaration date and exclusion agreement. Amend existing clause rather than replacing precedent unnecessarily |
| `SERVICE_AND_EXECUTION_CHECKLIST.md` | Any path | Separate record of service, evidence, tenant commitment, declaration and original executed documents |
| `FIELD_SCHEMA.json` | All templates | Defines mandatory/evidence-only fields and routing conditions |

**Do not mix**: Schedule 2 paragraph 7 and paragraph 8 are alternative declaration routes, not consecutive steps. The statutory declaration is not needed after a compliant 14-day notice and valid simple declaration. The complete prescribed Schedule 1 warning is reproduced within each declaration. Schedules 3 and 4 are for **agreements to surrender**, not for the grant of a tenancy.

**Formatting:** clean A4 Word forms, original mandatory warning text and a neutral place for party fields; no explanatory paragraphs are appended to the statutory notice itself. For matter-specific copies, preserve legal prose and statutory warning emphasis, fill only evidenced particulars, and do not leave inapplicable slash/conditional alternatives in an engrossment. The declarations have additional signature/jurat lines for practical execution; choose and confirm correct signing arrangements with the fee earner.
