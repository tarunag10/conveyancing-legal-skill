# HMLR pre-submission quality assurance

Matter / title(s) / reviewer / actual review date:

- [ ] Correct current official form and registration route (AP1, FR1, other as applicable)
- [ ] Parties, names, spelling, company numbers and capacities matched to original register/deeds
- [ ] Property, title number, extent and estate matched to actual official title plan/approved deed plans
- [ ] Executed transfer/charge/deeds and attestation method reviewed against current HMLR guidance
- [ ] Existing charges and DS1/e-DS1 or undertakings accounted for; incoming charge registered at correct rank
- [ ] Every applicable restriction: exact wording, certificate/consent/deed, source and evidence attached
- [ ] SDLT5 / WRA certificate where required, or documented non-notifiability basis
- [ ] Correct application type and verified current fee, reference, priority search and expiry
- [ ] Landlord/management notice/covenant/certificate requirements identified and evidenced if triggered
- [ ] Company MR01 completed/accepted within actual period if applicable, separately from HMLR registration
- [ ] All prescribed supporting evidence and lender reporting retained and reviewed
- [ ] Solicitor-authorised submission, actual submission reference and date recorded
- [ ] Requisitions: actual notice, date, response deadline, extensions, replies and HMLR outcome recorded
- [ ] Final official copies independently checked for registration of proprietor, estate, charge and priority

**Status:** checklist only; approval pending unless an actual lawyer signs off and source evidence is recorded. Not an AP1/FR1 form.
