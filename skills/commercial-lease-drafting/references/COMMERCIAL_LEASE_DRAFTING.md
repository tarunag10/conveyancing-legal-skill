# Commercial lease, rent deposit and LTA 1954 declaration drafting standard

**Scope:** solicitor-supervised English and Welsh commercial leases from actual Heads of Terms and a full precedent. Reusable templates included with the plugin, not a substitute for signed client instructions, current legislation, official Land Registry forms and title-plan verification.

## Authoritative sources to verify each time

- [Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, section 38A](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Eliz2/2-3/56/section/38A) and [Regulatory Reform (Business Tenancies) (England and Wales) Order 2003, Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/schedule/1/made) and [Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/schedule/2/made). Note paragraph 2/3 (14+ days/simple) and 4/8 (<14 days/statutory), paragraph 5 (notice/declaration reference in instrument), paragraph 6 (agreement/reference in instrument). Check commencement, version, service and the real binding date at the time of drafting.
- [HMLR PG64, prescribed clauses leases](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/prescribed-clauses-leases/practice-guide-64-prescribed-clauses-leases), especially required front position, LR4 property description, required headings, LR11 rights and schedules, and [PG25, leases: when to register](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/leases-when-to-register/practice-guide-25-leases-when-to-register).
- Land Registration Act 2002 section 27 and Land Registration Rules 2003 Schedule 1A, HMLR plan/lease registration guidance; Law of Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1989 section 1 and HMLR PG8 deed execution; Town and Country Planning (Use Classes) Order 1987 as amended for relevant permitted use; VAT/tax laws and lender/superior title requirements if relevant.

These links are **entry points**, not assertions of live verification by the package. The drafting agent must check official sources at point of use or label the legal review unverified.

## Integration with specialist LTA 1954 workflow

The installed plugin also contains `skills/lt1954-contracting-out/SKILL.md`, `knowledge/practice/LTA1954_CONTRACTING_OUT.md`, and `templates/lta1954/TEMPLATE_INDEX.md` with **prescribed Schedule 1 notice, Schedule 2 simple declaration and statutory declaration masters**. They control the **legal wording, notice content and procedure** when contracting out. The blank statutory declaration in this commercial-lease pack is a historical layout reference only and must never supersede a current verified prescribed form. For a new tenancy, do not apply the Schedules 3/4 surrender route.

## Source priority and change control

1. Extract *all* 17 or other numbered Heads of Terms, dates, parties, property/title, rent schedules, review, repair, permitted use, insurance, outgoings, service charge, break, alterations, alienation, 1954 exclusion and deposit costs. Compare against the entire old lease including annexes, schedules and executions.
2. Keep the precedent's full legal architecture and all unchanged provisions. Record each requested commercial variation and every necessary consequential modification by clause and definition. Separate legal error corrections from negotiated changes and obtain approval before changing parties' commercial allocation of risk.
3. Do not misrepresent an old registered lease as expired, surrendered or vacated. Check current title and occupancy before grant. An existing lease appearing on an old title copy is an issue, not proof of current status.
4. For missing data, leave a blank *visibly blank* write-in line in the deed. Put the precise inquiry in a separate **Outstanding Matters** section in the drafting report. An unknown legal mechanism or mandatory missing plan is not cured by a line: hold the document at review stage.
5. If the Heads omit rent deposit mechanism, select neither a trust nor landlord-ownership arrangement by default; obtain instructions. The master is merely a **specific historical starting point**.
6. If Heads do not provide a guarantor, do not impose a new guarantee merely because a precedent has a Schedule. If instructed to preserve the Schedule verbatim for review, keep it but flag it as conditional and do not execute until a guarantor is agreed.

## Professional layout and blank policy

- A4 page; consistent Calibri/Arial/Times as warranted by the actual precedent; 10-11 pt body text at a legible size; left aligned or justified body text as appropriate, with properly indented numbered clauses and lettered subclauses, no tab-spaced decorative alignment pretending to be a table.
- A separate cover, contents after the cover, prescribed clauses at the **front** of the registrable lease, then parties, definitions and grant, complete covenants, schedules, signature blocks, and annexed plan.
- Use actual Word styles for headings and body. Use headings with outline level/heading styles and either automatic TOC or bookmark-linked `PAGEREF` fields. Make page numbers reflect the **final** document; update/recalculate after formatting. Never leave a contents section listing a missing provision or carry old page numbers copied from the precedent.
- Numbering needs to be exact: principal clauses **1 through 50** in the packaged lease precedent and conditional guarantee Schedule paragraphs **1 through 7**. If negotiated omissions change numbering, regenerate relevant numbering, cross-references and contents from the resulting document. Do not force the generic count when the actual precedent differs.
- Keep short heading and following substantive text together, suppress widows/orphans where possible, keep witness and execution details on one page and correctly spaced, avoid overlong underlined fields which create forced wraps.
- Permissible blank: `________________________` or an empty Word content control; in deed text never use `[INSERT DATE]`, `[TO CONFIRM]`, `<party>`, `TBC`, `N/A?`, fake dates, bracketed explanatory notes or prose instructing someone to fill in a field. Retain ordinary substantive bracket punctuation where legally necessary; the ban targets **drafting artefacts**, not legitimate clause structure.
- A Word field/code is not an executed instrument. Do not insert a signature, swear date or mark the contracting-out process completed without evidence.

## Contracting out and related legal gates

- Establish whether the LTA 1954 applies. For a contracting-out case, check the landlord gave the notice in the statutory/substantially statutory form, its actual **service** and timing relative to commitment, the correct simple or statutory declaration and authority, the landlord/tenant agreement and required references in the lease. A statutory declaration requires an authorised person and cannot be made retrospectively after becoming contractually bound. If there is no valid procedure, do not represent that clauses 24-28 were excluded.
- Verify that the original lease's recital of **not less than 14 days** of notice is not combined with a short-notice statutory-declaration recital without considering the actual route. Correct discrepancies against the actual documents rather than reproducing the old 2017 wording.
- Insert only the actual permitted-use description derived from Heads and consents. Class E(b) relates to sale of food and drink for consumption mostly on the premises; classify a hot-food takeaway or mixed use only after investigating the actual planning position and use restrictions.
- Check title number(s), landlord legal ownership, notices/leases/restrictions, plans of parts, rights and reservations. LR4 must identify the property (with accurate plan); LR11 must reflect grants/reservations. A blank property description is fine *in a template* but is not an acceptable executed/registrable deed.

## Document-by-document checks

**Lease:** every Heads term; LR1-LR14 as relevant; all 50 baseline headings and contents; initial rent and stepped period vs quarterly due dates and payment on completion; one or several Review Dates as agreed; permitted use; repair/decorations; insurance/abatement; service charge; alterations/consents; assignment/subletting; law/costs; statutory procedure; plans; registration/execution.

**Rent Deposit Deed:** exact parties/landlord title/leased property; amount, deposit status, VAT, account and trust/ownership, drawdown, replenishment, interest, assignment/reversion, insolvency risk, time and condition for repayment, and compatible lease clause 6. Do not silently equate three months with a fixed figure or keep landlord ownership if a trust is instructed.

**Declaration:** Schedule 2 paragraph 8 statutory form only when applicable; first-person declarant and capacity; correct landlords; premises; warning notice; agreed exclusion; full text where prescribed; jurat with space for place/date/signature/person administering oath; no false statements of prior service or execution.

## Mandatory deliverable and acceptance tests

1. Produce three **separate** DOCX files when all three are actually applicable (or explain why not), never just an outline. Change-control and pending issues must be outside the deeds.
2. Mechanically compare the old lease headings and operative paragraphs to the new; report and reconcile any removed/changed provision not dictated by the Heads or essential legal corrections.
3. Verify **all** contents entries against corresponding real Word body headings (including schedules). Verify actual final pagination and cross-reference target existence. Check all LR clauses and executions. Refuse to claim quality assurance unless the checks were actually done.
4. Render DOCX to page images using available host tooling, examine **every page** for overflow, improper justification of long blank fields, missing text, isolated signature/witness blocks, wrong page numbers, empty pages and split headings. Iterate and re-render if needed.
5. Scan deed XML/text for banned drafting artefacts and stale matter names, title numbers, historic dates, addresses, amounts and author metadata. Internal blank lines are acceptable; actual unexplained missing dates remain in report and block final sign-off.
6. A supervising solicitor must approve legal choices, execution, official plans, warning notice, tax and registration before release. State draft status, not execution readiness.
