# Blank commercial leasing precedents (English and Welsh matters)

These are **editable DOCX templates**, not PDFs or executed instruments. They are user-supplied and adapted precedent structures, not Law Society or HM Land Registry official forms.

1. `COMMERCIAL_LEASE_MASTER.docx`: full 50-clause lease with real substantive wording, prescribed clauses LR1–LR14, a populated contents page with bookmark-linked page-number fields, a complete seven-section conditional Guarantee and Indemnity Schedule, three blank individual execution blocks and a blank Plan 1 page.
2. `RENT_DEPOSIT_DEED_MASTER.docx`: standalone associated deed. **Default legal structure is landlord ownership of the funds**, as in the supplied example. Not appropriate automatically for a trust or stakeholder deposit.
3. `STATUTORY_DECLARATION_1954_MASTER.docx`: conditional short-notice statutory declaration, following Schedule 2 paragraph 8 of the 2003 Order. The separate simple declaration route applies where the warning notice was served at least 14 days before the tenant becomes bound.

## Use

1. Read `skills/commercial-lease-drafting/SKILL.md` and `docs/COMMERCIAL_LEASE_DRAFTING.md` first.
2. Start with the actual supplied precedent where available. Otherwise duplicate these documents into **approved private matter storage**. They have no real client details. Replace the *blank lines* with verified names, amounts, dates, property and other information from the Heads of Terms and title/notice documents, never fake values.
3. Adapt all inapplicable standard clauses, including guarantor provisions, permitted use, rent escalation, rent review and deposit mechanism. Unknown legal choices require a separate solicitor approval, not a blank signature line.
4. Generate the contents anew after all amendments, check each referenced heading and the guarantee schedule, render and inspect all pages. Do not rely on visible cached field numbers after moving clauses.
5. Fill in official LR prescribed clauses accurately and attach a compliant final property plan. Contracting out needs a real notice, correct declaration route, and correct statutory recital. Leave execution and attestation blank until proper signing and swearing.

## Matter privacy

Do not save completed copies in this plugin package, source repository, examples or issue tickets. Store only sanitised precedent DOCX files. Do not package names, addresses, bank details or personal references from client files.
