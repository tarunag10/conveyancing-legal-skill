# Commercial lease and associated deeds drafting (solicitor-supervised)

Use this skill whenever the instruction is to prepare a **new or renewed commercial lease**, or any corresponding **rent deposit deed, 1954 Act declaration and lease-plan annex**, from Heads of Terms, an existing lease, correspondence and title materials. This skill is part of the existing `ew-conveyancing-legal` plugin, not a separate legal authority.

**Canonical rules:** read the plugin root `SKILL.md`, the controlling `core/` rules, `knowledge/workflows/ROUTER.md` and `knowledge/practice/COMMERCIAL_AND_TAX.md` **when available in the host**. Always read this skill's bundled `references/COMMERCIAL_LEASE_DRAFTING.md` (available via the installed skill file reader) and actual case documents. If canonical files cannot be loaded in this host, explicitly state which checks were not performed; this self-contained drafting procedure still applies and live law must be independently verified. Do not rely on a generic master where an actual precedent is supplied.

## Packaged complete editable Word starting points

- `assets/COMMERCIAL_LEASE_MASTER.docx` (also `templates/COMMERCIAL_LEASE_MASTER.docx` at plugin root): full 50-clause lease, prescribed clauses LR1-LR14, genuine contents page and page references, complete Schedule (Guarantee and Indemnity, paragraphs 1-7), execution and blank plan sheet.
- `assets/RENT_DEPOSIT_DEED_MASTER.docx` (also `templates/RENT_DEPOSIT_DEED_MASTER.docx` at plugin root): standalone commercial rent deposit deed with operative provisions and appropriate execution blocks.
- `assets/STATUTORY_DECLARATION_1954_MASTER.docx` (also `templates/STATUTORY_DECLARATION_1954_MASTER.docx` at plugin root): **conditional** statutory declaration for the Schedule 2 paragraph 4/8 route; contains uncompleted declarant and jurat lines.

These are **sanitised blank precedents** derived from a user-provided 2017 England commercial lease and revised corresponding drafts, **not** current prescribed official forms, model terms fit for every transaction or legal advice. They contain no identifiable matter details. Their repeated terms, two-landlord execution, deposit ownership mechanism, repair obligations, guarantee schedule, upward-only rent review and contracting-out clause are **starting points, not assumptions**. Alter/remove any inapplicable provision on evidence and solicitor instructions. Never state that a blank master is execution-ready.

## Must-follow workflow

1. **Evidence first.** Read *all* supplied Heads of Terms, precedent lease (including every schedule and plan), any historic deed, live title and related correspondence. Record parties, demised estate, title number, address, term, rent steps, VAT, dates, deposit, rent review, guarantor, security of tenure, repair, use, insurance, service charges, alienation, alterations, costs and special terms with page/paragraph evidence references. The Heads of Terms govern the agreed *commercial variations*; retain unrelated precedent terms unless legally defective or specifically superseded.
2. **Map every agreed term** from Heads of Terms to precise operative clause(s), definitions, prescribed clauses and ancillary documents. Use a change-control schedule outside the deeds. Identify contradictions and missing approvals. Do not infer an agreement from silence or insert negotiated obligations without authority.
3. **Select the base:** prefer the user-provided precedent; otherwise copy and adapt the appropriate complete DOCX master. Make a copy for the matter. Do not treat a skeleton or short summary as a lease. Keep consistent numbering and cross-references. Where there is no guarantor, remove the guarantor execution and guarantee obligations/schedule *only if instructed or clearly inapplicable*, and regenerate the contents; do not leave false guarantor covenants or a schedule listed but absent.
4. **Word document format:** create fully editable DOCX with A4 paper, ordinary UK commercial deed typography, genuine Word heading styles, stable clause/subclause numbering and hanging indents, professional cover, front contents, prescribed clauses, substantive operative paragraphs, schedules, correctly spaced execution blocks, page numbers and a plan annex when required. Content in the table of contents must actually exist in the document. Build/update TOC **after** the last substantive edit using heading/bookmark-linked page references; never paste old page numbers or claim a link works without checking it.
5. **Blanks, not drafting commentary:** within the operative instruments, any genuinely unknown name, address, title, execution date, notice date, amount or description uses **unlabelled write-in lines or an empty field** (`________________________`), never bracketed instructions, `[TO CONFIRM]`, `TBC`, `PLACEHOLDER`, example data, explanations or invented facts. For unknown *legal choices* (deposit mechanism, service charge basis, rights, 1954 Act route) do **not** disguise the absence of agreement using blanks; hold that term unresolved in the **separate** drafting report and do not present an execution-ready instrument.
6. **1954 Act gate:** when contracting out is instructed, **also load and treat as controlling** `skills/lt1954-contracting-out/SKILL.md`, `knowledge/practice/LTA1954_CONTRACTING_OUT.md`, and the prescribed forms under `templates/lta1954/`. The bundled commercial statutory declaration master is a layout precedent only; the verified current Schedule 2 form from that specialist skill takes priority for actual declaration drafting. Explicitly determine whether security of tenure is to be excluded. Verify the Schedule 1 warning notice, service, date the tenant first becomes contractually bound, the relevant Schedule 2 **simple declaration (>=14 days)** or **statutory declaration (<14 days)**, the correct declarant/authority and deed recitals under section 38A and the Regulatory Reform (Business Tenancies) (England and Wales) Order 2003. Only use the packaged statutory template for the applicable statutory route. The statutory declaration must be made before the relevant commitment, before an authorised person; never indicate it is already sworn. If in doubt, withhold execution-ready certification and refer for solicitor approval.
7. **Title, plan and registration gate:** check the current register, existing lease/occupation, joint ownership, restrictions, superior consents, correct demise and an HMLR-compliant plan. For registrable leases apply current Land Registration Rules prescribed LR clauses (LR1-LR14) and HMLR PG64/PG25 and applicable plan guidance, without inventing register data. A blank plan annex is **not** an acceptable completed registration plan.
8. **Inter-document consistency:** check lease and deed parties; property and title; grant/term; initial/stepped/quarterly rent and review; rent deposit figure, VAT treatment, ownership/trust mechanism, draws, replenishment and return; 1954 notices and declarations; dates and signature capacities. Inconsistency or unsafe commercial assumption is a blocker.
9. **Quality gate before delivery:** programmatically enumerate every intended heading, paragraph and schedule in the *source* and *draft*; inspect every clause referenced in the contents, verify schedule paragraph 1-7 if the full guarantee schedule is retained, all clause cross-references and execution blocks. Render each DOCX to pages and **visually inspect every page** for broken tables, displaced text, stranded words/blank lines, orphans, missing pages and bad pagination. Do not deliver a document merely because file generation succeeded. Re-render after fixes. If tools for export/render are unavailable, state exactly what was not performed.
10. **Deliver:** separate clean DOCX files for each applicable document, plus a standalone Heads-of-Terms-to-clause change-control report, outstanding evidence and approval list, and a succinct execution/registration preflight. No legal commentary inside deed provisions. Confirm expressly whether the documents are drafts or approved, and do not sign, swear, serve, lodge or claim completion.
