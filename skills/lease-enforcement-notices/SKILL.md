# Enforcement and termination notices

Read root skill, `knowledge/practice/LEASE_ENFORCEMENT_NOTICES.md`, actual signed lease/contract and applicable authority. Choose neutral DOCX in `templates/notices/enforcement/` only after checking recipient, legally operative clause, notice service terms, trigger, deadline and entitlement. Section 146 is high-risk: only a **non-serviceable preflight worksheet** is bundled; settle bespoke notice under Law of Property Act 1925 s146 and other prerequisites with supervisor. Section 27 and s29B need separate 1954 Act checks. Do not assume automatic termination, forfeiture or completion from letter preparation. Verify current law and relevant terms before release.
