# LTA 1954: contracting out of security of tenure (new business tenancies)

**Read first:** repository `../../SKILL.md`, `../../core/OPERATING_STANDARD.md`, `../../core/EVIDENCE_AND_RISK.md`, `../../knowledge/practice/LTA1954_CONTRACTING_OUT.md`, and `../../templates/lta1954/TEMPLATE_INDEX.md`. For a lease review also read `../../knowledge/practice/COMMERCIAL_AND_TAX.md` and the actual heads of terms and draft lease. At point of use check the authoritative 2003 Order and relevant current amendments; a bundled form is a dated precedent, not evidence that the law or facts are verified.

## Automatic routing

Activate this skill whenever a user requests:
- a business lease **outside the 1954 Act**, **contracted out**, **excluded security of tenure**, or an **unprotected lease**;
- a Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 **section 38A** or **sections 24-28** warning notice, simple declaration, statutory declaration or contracting-out lease provision;
- preparation or review of a commercial lease, agreement for lease or underlease whose heads of terms specify contracting out;
- checks on a proposed service/declaration timeline, tenant's signed statement or defective exclusion clause.

For a generic commercial lease, first check the intended security-of-tenure position; if contracting out is instructed or apparent in the source terms, use this skill. Do not *assume* that every commercial lease is to be contracted out. If the user asks for the **surrender of an existing business tenancy**, follow **Schedules 3 and 4** of the 2003 Order (a different procedure); do not reuse the Schedules 1/2 grant-of-tenancy forms or declare retrospective contracting out possible.

## Mandatory selection

1. Classify new lease / underlease / agreement for lease versus surrender, England/Wales jurisdiction, prospective tenancy and whether Part II protection could apply. Identify the proposed landlord(s), tenant, demised premises and when the tenant will **first become contractually bound**, not merely the anticipated completion date.
2. Check parties' clear instruction/agreement to exclude sections 24-28. Do not invent tenant consent or declare that procedure has happened.
3. **Always** select the prescribed Schedule 1 notice for a new tenancy. Give it *before* the tenant is bound. Populate only the specified **To/From names and addresses** supported by matter documents. Do not add property description, signature or date into the prescribed notice; record service details separately.
4. From **evidenced** effective service and earliest contractual commitment dates, choose Schedule 2 paragraph **7 simple declaration** if the notice was served **at least 14 days** beforehand; otherwise Schedule 2 paragraph **8 statutory declaration**, which must itself be duly made before the tenant is bound. If dates are unknown, label both routes **pending selection**, not either route completed. Never backdate a notice or declaration.
5. In either route, retain the complete prescribed notice **reproduced within the declaration**, not merely referenced. Check the correct declarant (tenant or duly authorised person), name/address, landlord identity, premises and term commencement; resolve the prescribed singular/plural alternatives and conditional authorisation paragraph appropriately. For a statutory declaration arrange an authorised person to administer it and complete the jurat. Never represent it as sworn merely because a draft exists.
6. Include or endorse in the **instrument creating the tenancy** references to actual notice and declaration **and** the operative agreement excluding sections 24-28, under Schedule 2 paragraphs 5 and 6. Select the clause alternative for simple or statutory declaration and fill dates from evidence only. If a binding agreement for lease is proposed, complete the procedure before that agreement is made.
7. Confirm proof of service, identities, dates, correct forms, authority (corporate/agent), service method and execution. Check the prior occupational tenancy / surrender independently; new-lease exclusion does not extinguish an existing lease.
8. Return reusable editable DOCX drafts, a concise matter-specific chronological checklist and blockers. Mark **DRAFT / NOT SERVED / NOT SWORN / NOT EXECUTED** until independently evidenced; a document template cannot complete the statutory procedure.
