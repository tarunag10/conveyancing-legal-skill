# Lender-specific CoT / RoT document and submission navigator

For solicitor-supervised England and Wales conveyancing. Load the repository's canonical `../../SKILL.md`, `../../core/OPERATING_STANDARD.md`, `../../core/EVIDENCE_AND_RISK.md`, and `../../knowledge/practice/LENDERS_AND_ILA.md`; those instructions control everything here. Also read `../../knowledge/lenders/README.md` for decision gates and look up **the matching lender and exact product/representation** in `../../knowledge/lenders/lender-directory.json`. Use `../../templates/LENDER_COT_ROUTING_AND_SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` to record results if the user is managing a matter.

For each requested lender give: (1) lender/brand and evidence scope; (2) whether a CoT, eCoT or RoT applies; (3) official document source link; (4) only *verified* accepted submission route, or expressly **UNVERIFIED**; (5) case and panel access prerequisites; (6) source-defined minimum funding notice with units or unknown; (7) official lender/portal contacts where established; (8) legal/submission blockers; and (9) direct official source links and research date.

**Status discipline:** `verified_document_only` does NOT mean submit through LMS; `verified_directory_or_panel` does NOT establish a CoT/eCoT; `lookup_only` means no route determined. Even `verified_submission` is a dated public snapshot, not authority to submit a certificate on a live case. Current mortgage offer, lender instruction, applicable UK Finance England and Wales Part 1/2/3 and actual lender communication prevail. Confirm all lender requirements and any written response to reportable defects before issuing an unqualified CoT. User must arrange qualified human solicitor approval, signing and submission. Never sign, certify title, file or release funds on the model's behalf.

Do not treat Rely as Kent Reliance, HSBC UK as HSBC Private Bank, Barclays retail as commercial, NatWest retail as commercial, or Halifax as Lloyds; they can have different CoT forms and routes. For Together determine which firm is instructed to prepare the lender Report on Title, rather than assigning the same role to all borrower solicitors.

If a particular lender is absent, research its live direct official solicitors page, its current UK Finance Handbook response (questions 4.5b, 10.2b and 1.11) and the actual instruction letter; return a clearly provisional route rather than guessing. Never tell the user an eCoT was submitted unless an authorised human/connected case system shows confirmed acceptance.

**Matter integration:** when CoT/RoT questions concern a live completion timeline or multiple properties, load `../../skills/matter-workbench/SKILL.md` and build a source-specific condition and drawdown record. Never treat an offline directory lookup as a submitted eCoT.
