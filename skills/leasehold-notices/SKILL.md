# Leasehold notifications and consents

Read root skill and `knowledge/practice/LEASEHOLD_NOTICE_DRAFTING.md`. Read actual lease, current title restrictions and freeholder/agent instructions before selecting `templates/notices/leasehold/*.docx`. Notice of transfer, notice of charge, consent to assign/alter and certificate of compliance are separate from HMLR registrations and do not create an automatic consent. Determine actual parties, service address, certified documents, contractual notice period, fees, method and receipt. Do not treat a generic landlord consent request as formal approval. Only populate evidence-backed facts, leave other spaces blank, and give a separate solicitor review checklist.
