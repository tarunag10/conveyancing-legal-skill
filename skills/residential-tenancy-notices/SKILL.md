# Residential notices and tenancy regime selection

Read root skill, `knowledge/practice/RESIDENTIAL_NOTICE_ROUTING.md`, actual tenancy agreement and `templates/notices/residential/READ_ME_FIRST.md`. First distinguish England private assured tenancies from Welsh occupation contracts, social housing and historic transitional notices. **For new England private-sector notices after 1 May 2026, obtain the current official Form 3A or Form 4A from https://www.gov.uk/guidance/assured-tenancy-forms**; the two DOCX files are drafting worksheets only. Official Form 3A and legal grounds wording are published and may change; do not reconstruct from memory or use historic Section 21/Form 6A for new cases. Validate possession grounds, actual notice period, landlord preconditions, and 2026 transitional dates. For s47/48 LTA 1987 verify Part VI applicability, landlord name and England/Wales service address. No service, rent rise or possession is effected merely by draft preparation.
