# Business tenancy notices: renewal, information and ending

Load plugin root `SKILL.md`, `core/OPERATING_STANDARD.md`, `knowledge/workflows/ROUTER.md`, `knowledge/practice/BUSINESS_TENANCY_NOTICES.md`, `templates/notices/business/READ_ME_FIRST.md` and catalogue `OFFICIAL_FORMS_1_TO_17.json`. If a new tenancy is proposed to be contracted out, also load `skills/lt1954-contracting-out/SKILL.md` and do **not** substitute renewal forms.

Always choose correct prescribed **Form 1–17**, obtain official current wording at point of use, fill evidenced facts in original form; DO NOT serve a `FORM_XX_PREPARATION_WORKSHEET.docx` (this is an internal fact sheet, not a statutory form). Determine 6–12 month notice window, actual s25 or s26 date, correct section 30 grounds, s40 information and s27 termination route. For court/tribunal deadlines check s29A, timely signed s29B written extension and, if relevant, 30 July 2026 telecom jurisdiction update. Do not assume general case law from form title.

Never guess notice validity, sign, serve or file. Report exact actual source and version and unresolved blockers for supervising solicitor. Render any created Word document and compare operative form to official document, with case materials stored privately.
