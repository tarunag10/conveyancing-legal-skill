# Evidence, issue taxonomy and risk grading

## Levels (do not use colour alone)

| Level | Meaning | Default action |
|---|---|---|
| R0 Routine | Evidenced administrative task, no identified substantive defect | Track to completion |
| R1 Enquiry | Material information missing, discrepancy or answer required | Raise targeted enquiry; reserve position |
| R2 Material title or financial issue | Potential impairment of use, value, marketability or remedy; indemnity proposal needs specialist scrutiny | Escalate to supervising conveyancer; assess lender report |
| R3 Lender-reportable | The mortgage instructions **may** require notice or approval; no automatic lender approval inferred | Report through authorised route and await written decision if required |
| R4 Completion blocker | Evidenced outstanding legal/contractual/security/funding prerequisite, or unacceptable exposure | Do not certify readiness or treat completion as authorised |

These categories are not interchangeable; one issue can be both R2 and R3, and either can escalate to R4. R3 is a reporting classification, not an assertion that a handbook provision applies to all lenders.

## Evidence weights

- E0 Not provided or unreadable.
- E1 Client, agent or third-party assertion (e.g., 'charge vacated', 'road adopted').
- E2 Dated copy of primary instrument, register, formal correspondence or application acknowledgement, with attribution.
- E3 Primary instrument plus independent official confirmation or probative registration result. Authenticity and legal sufficiency remain subject to review.

## Inference traps

1. 'Vacated' in an email does not prove discharge. Inspect signed endorsed deed, DS1/e-DS1/cancellation record or acceptable release, and confirm impact of historic unregistered charges.
2. Red road colouring on a map does not, by itself, prove highway adoption or a legally enforceable right to access the precise extent of land.
3. A boundary drawn on a title plan does not give a definitive boundary unless registered as such; compare physical and filed plans.
4. 'All monies' and further charges can defeat an assumption that a redemption relates to just one facility.
5. The absence of an obvious adverse entry is not proof of no overriding interest, overriding lease, Land Charge, restriction or pre-registration event.
6. A director's guarantee is not validated by a witnessed signature image. Confirm capacity, execution and independently documented advice as instructed.
7. An auction contract's obligation to complete is not erased by outstanding mortgage arrangements; analyse notice/default and interest separately.
8. Tax status is not set solely by the contract labels 'VAT', 'TOGC' or 'no VAT'.
9. The registration/official search priority expires; filing an AP1 does not establish its acceptance or registration.
10. Building Safety Act protections depend on qualifying facts and appropriate documents; do not substitute old EWS1 claims or generic marketing text.
