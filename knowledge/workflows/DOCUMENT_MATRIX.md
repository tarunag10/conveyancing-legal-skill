# Matter document and evidence matrix

Use this as an applicability aid, not a universal list. Select the matter profile and record each item as unassessed, requested, received, reviewed, missing, unreadable, superseded or not applicable with rationale. Record a document ID, source, date, relevant title/party and page or clause locator; link findings, conflicts, owner and actual deadline. Keep client documents and completed records in the firm’s approved case system, not this repository.

| Matter or trigger | Evidence to consider, as applicable |
|---|---|
| All matters | Instructions and authority, party/ownership evidence, sale memorandum, title numbers, key dates, conflict and approved CDD references, source-of-funds trail, client decisions and actual lender instructions |
| Purchase / sale | Contract and incorporated conditions, register/plan and filed deeds, seller forms obtained under licence, search results, enquiries and replies, transfer, redemption and completion statements |
| Remortgage / transfer of equity | Existing title and charges, lender offer and instructions, redemption figures, transfer/charge documents, ownership and trust instructions, tax assessment and consents |
| Leasehold / managed estate | Lease, variations and plans, management pack and accounts, insurance, works, service charges, consents and notices, relevant building-safety evidence |
| New build / shared ownership | Development title and plans, contract and incentives, planning/building evidence, warranties, estate and management documents, lease and scheme approvals where relevant |
| Auction | Complete legal pack, incorporated and special conditions, title and searches, deposit, buyer obligations, actual completion and notice terms |
| Unregistered | Epitome, root and subsequent deeds, plans, Land Charges results, identity and first-registration evidence; use the lost-deeds procedure where relevant |
| Commercial / mixed use | Leases, occupational information, VAT and option-to-tax evidence, TOGC facts, planning, environmental and funding documentation; obtain specialist input as needed |
| Probate, attorney, trust, insolvency or company | Evidence of appointment, authority and capacity, relevant instruments and consents, ownership/control, company and insolvency checks as applicable |

For plans or scans, record what was actually inspected and its limitations. Text extraction is not visual confirmation of boundaries, signatures, deeds or identity.
