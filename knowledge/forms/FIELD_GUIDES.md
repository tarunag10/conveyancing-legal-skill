# High-priority prescribed form field guides (references only)

**Never substitute this guide for the current HMLR form and instructions.** Publisher date and last-checked date are stored in `catalogue.json`; binary attachments and exact blank Word layout must be checked when preparing individual forms. These are paraphrased workflow prompts, not a reproduction of prescribed language.

## TR1 whole transfer: official HMLR guidance updated 23 January 2024

| Panel | Populate only from | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Title numbers | Current official title(s) actually transferred | Distinguish related freehold/leasehold titles; if unregistered leave blank |
| 2 Property | Contract, title description, filed plan | Do not substitute marketing address for legal land extent |
| 3 Date | Actual completion confirmation | Leave **blank until completion**; not exchange date |
| 4 Transferor(s) | Current register or legal authority of successor / PR | Exact full names, corporate status, power of attorney evidence |
| 5 Transferee(s) | Signed client instructions and CDD | Full legal names, company number/other required corporate details |
| 6 Address(es) for service | Express instructions from each transferee | Verify current valid correspondence address(es) |
| 7 Transfer | PRESCRIBED text | **Do not add to or amend** |
| 8 Consideration | Contract price and settlement terms | Distinguish price/other consideration/gift; avoid assumption |
| 9 Title guarantee | Agreed contract and special conditions | Full/limited guarantee according to legal instructions; any modification agreed and correctly recorded |
| 10 Trust declaration | Confirmed beneficial instructions | Do not guess joint tenancy/tenancy in common; consider JO and Form A restriction |
| 11 Additional provisions | Approved agreed covenants and qualified instructions | Never invent easements, releases or indemnities |
| 12 Execution | Genuine signatures and prescribed execution block | Correct witnesses/corporate authority; no false attestation |

## TP1

Prove transfer of PART and inspect plan for orientation, extent, coloured areas, rights granted and reserved, covenants, guarantee and execution. Never substitute a TR1 or recreate statutory wording. Check HMLR PG21 and PG8. Confirm retained land has access, services and lender partial discharge. Official: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-titles-part-transfer-tp1

## AP1

Record application types in intended priority, correct title/parties and fee method, transferor/transferee identity, deeds/certificates, restrictions, SDLT5 or other relevant tax clearance and priority search. Check evidential and conveyancer certification requirements and supporting schedules. Official: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/change-the-register-ap1

## FR1 and DL

Confirm claim basis and title class, title identification, accurate plan, complete historical epitome and **DL in duplicate**, original supporting deeds as required, lease/charges and application fees. For lost deeds use PG2, not a generic affidavit. Official: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/first-application-registration-fr1 and HMLR PG1.

## DS1/DS2/CH1/OS1/OS2/K15

- **DS1:** identify precise charge and chargee, obtain acceptable chargee execution/release. **DS2:** application to cancel entry where applicable. Check if e-DS1 occurs electronically and whether part-release needs DS3.
- **CH1:** lender document and borrower execution must match secured property, sum/all-monies and registrability; don't replace lender's own approved charge without permission.
- **OS1/OS2:** correct scope whole/part, day list and 30 working-day protection. Check official search result for intervening adverse applications.
- **K15:** search *names of unregistered estate owners*, former county/name variations and years; from 1855; includes bankruptcy search. K16 bankruptcy only. Review certificate against actual name and time requested.

## Companies House MR01, MR04 and MR05: different register from HM Land Registry

**MR01:** Register qualifying company charge with Companies House within **21 days beginning on the day after creation**. Confirm legal creation date (including delivery of a deed, escrow or other issue), not just the printed signature date. Submit correct company identity, particulars and a certified copy of the instrument signed by the charging company; official guidance says MR01 authentication requires a printed name and does **not** require a signature on the MR01 form. Check current filing fee and channel at the official page; do not copy an old printed fee. Late acceptance can require a court extension. Do not confuse Companies House MR01 with a Land Registry application to register charge CH1/AP1 or with actual HMLR priority.

**MR04/MR05:** Companies House entries of satisfaction or release are different from release/discharge of an HMLR land charge; assess each register separately. Official: https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/forms-for-mortgages-and-loans

MR01 source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-particulars-of-a-charge-mr01/how-to-complete-paper-form-mr01 (updated 5 March 2026).

## HMRC SDLT return and Welsh LTT return

**SDLT1/2/3/4 and SDLT5:** From HMRC guidance published 26 June 2026, solicitor and conveyancer representatives should file SDLT electronically through the HMRC Stamp Taxes service; unrepresented buyers may use the genuine ordered SDLT1 paper return and any appropriate supplementary forms. The SDLT5 is an **HMRC-issued return certificate**, not an application form to be fabricated. A valid return does not determine title or relieve the conveyancer of tax/calculation checks. Confirm actual notifiability and the generally applicable 14-day rule from effective date.

Sources: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-send-a-stamp-duty-land-tax-return and https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sdlt-guide-for-completing-paper-sdlt1-return

**Wales:** Conveyancers use the WRA professional online Land Transaction Tax service to file and obtain a WRA certificate. An unrepresented person obtains the paper return route from WRA. Confirm Welsh property, return requirement and generally applicable 30-calendar-day period and exceptions. Source: https://www.gov.wales/file-land-transaction-tax
