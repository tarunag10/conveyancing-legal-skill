# Priority form selection and verification

These are navigation prompts, not approved panel-by-panel instructions or a binary form library. Check current form, guidance, execution, evidence, fees and submission route for the actual matter. Record publisher page and revision, actual filename/hash only after inspection, check date and reviewer using `templates/SOURCE_REVIEW_RECORD.md`. The following links were checked on 2 November 2026; form binaries and individual panels were not independently validated.

| Form | Potential use and review focus | Official starting point |
|---|---|---|
| AP1 | Change a registered title; reconcile applications, parties, documents, restrictions, identity and fee | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/change-the-register-ap1 |
| TR1 | Transfer whole of registered title; check parties, extent, consideration, trusts and execution | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-titles-whole-transfer-tr1 |
| TP1 | Transfer part; inspect plan, rights and retained land, and consider lender release | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/registered-titles-part-transfer-tp1 |
| FR1 / DL | First registration; examine title evidence, plan and applicable original/copy requirements | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/first-application-registration-fr1 |
| CH1 | Legal charge; check lender’s approved instrument and requirements | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/legal-charges-registration-ch1 |
| DS1 | Discharge of registered charge; confirm chargee and accepted release route | https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mortgage-cancellation-of-entries-for-lenders-ds1 |
| ID1 / ID2 | HMLR identity evidence for individuals / corporate bodies where required; assess representation and exceptions | https://www.gov.uk/guidance/completing-forms-id1-and-id2 |

For HMLR identity requirements consult https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/evidence-of-identity-conveyancers/practice-guide-67-evidence-of-identity-conveyancers. These are distinct from AML/CDD obligations. Law Society and other proprietary forms require authorised access and must not be reproduced without permission. See https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/property/transaction-forms. Verify edition and applicability at point of use.
