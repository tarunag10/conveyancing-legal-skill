# Conveyancing matter packs and scenario coverage

Use with the relevant profile, document matrix and specific title, lease, auction, lender, registration and tax modules. Establish whether acting for buyer, seller, borrower, lender, landlord or tenant before using a template. Record each document's version, recipient, source, date and page/clause locator. Classify a missing document as outstanding; do not infer that an unanswered question has been resolved. Check live law, applicable practice and actual lender instructions at point of use.

## Sale and purchase

For a seller, assemble authority to sell, title, property details, licensed seller forms as applicable, occupier and tenancy position, existing security, redemption arrangements, management documents and contract issues. Track responses, undertakings, approval and post-completion obligations. For a buyer, compare contract, title and plans, seller replies, searches, occupation, finance, funding, tax and client reports. Record deviations, enquiries, advice and actual decisions. Use `templates/SALE_PACK_REVIEW.md` or `templates/PURCHASE_PACK_REVIEW.md`.

## Leasehold and managed estate

Distinguish the demise, superior titles and relevant transfer, lease, variations, plans, estate documents and management structure. Record rent/review, service charge, funds, insurance, repairs, planned works, arrears, disputes, restrictions, certificates, notices, fees and relevant building safety evidence. A managing agent's reply is an identified source, not automatic proof. Review the current licensed LPE/TA forms supplied by the user without reproducing proprietary forms. Use `templates/LEASE_MANAGEMENT_REVIEW.md`.

## Auctions and finance

For both seller and buyer, identify exact legal pack edition, special conditions, title and search scope, fees, deposit, binding event and actual contract timetable. Distinguish pre-bid questions from obligations after exchange; check actual contract terms. For bridging and other facilities review the executed/issued versions of the offer, facility, security and guarantees. Map each drawdown and registration condition to a clause, evidence, responsible person and actual lender response. Use `templates/AUCTION_PACK_MATRIX.md` and `templates/FACILITY_REVIEW.md`.

## Evidence, declarations and gifts

For statements, declarations and gifts use `EVIDENCE_GIFTS_AND_LOANS.md` and `templates/EVIDENCE_GIFT_LOAN_REVIEW.md`. `templates/DECLARATION_FACT_SCHEDULE.md` is a fact-gathering schedule only: the correct form, wording and execution method depend on the recipient and facts. HMLR statements of truth for registration have their own guidance: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statements-of-truth/practice-guide-73-statements-of-truth. Deeds should be assessed against current HMLR guidance where applicable: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/execution-of-deeds/practice-guide-8-execution-of-deeds.

## Plans, scans and limitations

Use `templates/PLAN_REVIEW.md` and record physical inspection, source, edition, scale, colouring and unclear markings. HMLR explains general boundaries in https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hm-land-registry-plans-boundaries-pg40s3. Neither OCR nor a title plan alone proves an exact boundary, scale, execution or signature authenticity. Keep OCR opt-in and check it against originals. Refer survey and specialist legal questions appropriately.

## Tax

Identify jurisdiction, effective date, consideration, transaction type, higher-rate and relief questions, and actual evidence before proposing a tax position. Simple band calculations are illustrative; no rate or date-only stop establishes liability or clearance. Welsh higher-rate transitional treatment and other exceptions need review of the actual facts and current sources. Use the jurisdiction's official calculator and guidance and document a qualified review.
