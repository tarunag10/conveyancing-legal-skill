# Auction purchases and sales

**Load when:** auction pack, auction memorandum, special conditions, general conditions, contractual notice to complete, default interest or fees. Never call a contract term statutory merely because it appears in a standard auction document.

## Immediate purchaser triage

Request complete auction lot catalogue, special conditions, incorporated general conditions **with edition**, executed auction memorandum, amendments/announcements, actual exchange date/time, vendor replies, title documents, searches and tenancy documents. Determine whether the auction formed an immediate binding contract, and identify any conditions varying ordinary completion or title investigation entitlements.

Review clause by clause for: property identity/title and exclusions; full/limited/no title guarantee; seller's covenants for title and carve-outs under LP(MP)A 1994; known or latent defects; scope of replies/ability to raise requisitions; completion date; daily/contractual interest rate and how computed; seller’s costs, reimbursements, buyer premium and additional lot charges; property insurance risk; tenant possession and arrears; deposit forfeiture; remedies; notice to complete service modes and deemed service; VAT/TOGC condition; chattels; apportionments; missing searches; road access and seller title discharge; no-reliance clauses and auction-specific indemnities.

## Time and remedies

**Contract precedence gate:** do not reuse standard residential 10-working-day notice periods or deadline rules without the operative incorporated clauses. Map events in date order with source references, exact time of day, whether working/calendar days, service mechanism, bank and public holiday impact, time of essence, and consequences of lender delay. Explain if a purported notice to complete appears defective or if different versions of conditions conflict. The contract may provide a limited right to raise requisitions; preserve the buyer's position while seeking an extension.

If financing is delayed, advise the supervising lawyer to disclose only what is appropriate to the seller solicitors, request a specific extension expressly subject to agreed terms, track whether the notice is valid and whether interest/default costs continue. Do not imply that an extension exists absent written agreement.

## Unregistered auction property

Read full epitome, all historic charges and endorsements, Land Charges search chain, statutory rights and access; apply `TITLE_UNREGISTERED.md`. Auction purchase does not remove land registration obligations. Check first-registration financing and any lender requirement to see a mortgage-only environmental indemnity; premium and cover terms must be approved rather than assumed.

## VAT risk

An auction special condition may require the purchaser to pay VAT if chargeable, reimburse costs, or certify TOGC status. Treat VAT as a **tax-law question** in addition to contractual risk. Obtain seller's option-to-tax evidence and buyer's intended use/registrability; TOGC cannot simply be excluded by deleting a special condition if statutory requirements apply. Refer to `COMMERCIAL_AND_TAX.md`.

## Outputs

1. Auction risk report sorted R4 to R0 with each special-condition clause, operative law, precise potential exposure and proposed amendment/insurance.
2. Contractual date-and-cost schedule with version of conditions, calculation basis, known and missing inputs.
3. Seller solicitor targeted requisitions, documentary evidence list and lender title report items.
4. Funding and completion blocker report: deposit paid, lender advance status, completion balance, notice/default status and outstanding title cures.
5. Post-completion requirements distinct from contractual obligations.
