# Property searches: orders, priority, result review

**Trigger:** local authority searches, title priority, OS1/OS2/OS3, SIM, official copies OC1/OC2, Land Charges K15, bankruptcy K16, LLC1, CON29/CON29O, environmental, drainage, highway/adoption, title/plan or search results. Load `templates/searches/SEARCH_ROUTING_CATALOGUE.json` and the relevant worksheet. **No actual official search is conducted by opening a template.**

* OS1 = whole title or pending first registration priority; OS2 = part priority (land plan); OS3 = without priority. Confirm searched title, proprietor, actual search-from date, applicant, disposition protected and the issued priority period against pending registration. **An OS3 gives no registration priority.** HMLR official PG12: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/official-searches-and-outline-applications/practice-guide-12-official-searches-and-outline-applications .
* SIM = search index map to identify registered title number(s), not registration, title evidence or priority. Official PG10 updated 20 July 2026: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/official-searches-of-the-index-map/practice-guide-10-official-search-of-the-index-map .
* OC1 = certified copies of register / plan; OC2 = filed deeds and instruments. Verify official stamped register timestamp and title; title plans show general boundaries. A search result is not a title guarantee.
* K15 for relevant unregistered Land Charges, K16 bankruptcy-only name searches. Verify exact legal names and adverse entries by evidence; never equate a matching name alone with debtor identity. HMLR PG63: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-land-charges-registers-and-agricultural-credits-register-registration-official-search-office-copy-and-cancellation-pg63/practice-guide-63-land-charges-applications-for-registration-official-search-office-copy-and-cancellation .
* LLC1 = local-land-charges record, check whether HMLR service has taken over local authority register. CON29 = local authority standard enquiries, CON29O = optional questions. Do not bundle or reproduce licensed Law Society CON29/CON29O form text. HMLR PG79: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/local-land-charges-pg79/local-land-charges . Obtain current licensed form or approved provider.
* Environmental / drainage / highways: select competent provider, jurisdiction, scope, search plan and reliance. Track contamination, drainage connected/adopted, public highway extent, road adoption and access separately. Searches or indemnity insurance do not substitute for legal judgment.

For each result generate a factual source-identified report with search ID/version, land and parties, result date, adverse entries, enquiry/owner, lender notification if applicable, planned or actual resolution and sign-off. Do not auto-set clear/complete. All cost and authority decisions remain supervised.
