# Leasehold due diligence and building safety

**Load when:** leasehold flats, shared ownership, management pack, LPE1, enfranchisement, lease extension, high-rise buildings and insurance/service-charge enquiries.

## Leaseholder checklist

Analyse the full lease and every variation: date, original parties, term and start, ground rent schedule/review, rent review triggers, demise, parking/storage rights, access/services, repairing covenant (internal/structural), shared roofs/structure, alterations, assignment/sublet restrictions, lender consent, landlord rights of entry, forfeiture, tenant costs, notices, alienation and registration certificates. Identify restrictive and unexpected wording precisely.

Obtain completed current licensed TA7 with TA6 where relevant, LPE1/management enquiries, last three years service-charge and ground rent demands/receipts (as relevant), budget/anticipated major works, accounts, reserve funds, dispute/litigation, insurance schedule, fire risk assessment and landlord/freeholder/management company structure. Check any absent shareholder/RTM/management shares, stock transfer forms, membership and conveyancing restriction certificates.

Law Society TA6 **sixth edition** and TA7 **fifth edition** apply for new CQS matters instructed 30 March 2026 onward, with limited transitional use for previously instructed cases. Both are proprietary; this repository does not copy them. (Law Society official guidance verified 27 October 2026.)

## Building Safety Act 2022 (England)

Not all blocks or leasehold properties are covered in the same way. Establish height/stories, relevant building status, qualifying lease criteria, original relevant landlord/developer and date of grant, property value and principal residence/ownership history, remediation defect and evidence, landlord contribution condition, and certificate availability. Request correct **landlord's certificate** and **leaseholder deed of certificate** as relevant. Consult official BSA leaseholder protections guidance; a landlord request may require deed of certificate within the specified period. Never claim protection or enforceability without applying the actual statutory criteria. Inspect remediation funding/works and service charge exposure; check lender instructions separately.

Treat an EWS1 form, fire safety report or generic building safety letter as distinct evidence, not a complete substitute for statutory certificates. England building safety measures do not automatically extend to Wales in identical form. Flag any Welsh scheme/regulatory differences for verification.

## Leasehold reform commencement gate

The Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 has commenced in part and prospectively changes other areas. Official England leasehold toolkit states two-year qualification abolition live from January 2025 and RTM improvements live from March 2025; important enfranchisement valuation changes (e.g., 990-year extensions/marriage value) remained not brought into force as stated in September 2026 government explanatory material. Verify exact provision, commencement order and date for **each transaction** before giving statutory notice or valuation advice. Be especially careful of transitional provisions and Wales extent.

## Output

Provide lease particulars, financial obligations and arrears, consents/restrictions, building-safety applicability evidence, proposed enquiries, lender constraints, statutory timing, registration actions and a solicitor-reviewed completion decision. Distinguish disputed demands from balances actually payable.
