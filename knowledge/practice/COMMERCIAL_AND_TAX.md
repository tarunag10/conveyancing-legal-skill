# Commercial property, VAT/TOGC, SDLT and Welsh LTT

**Load when:** commercial transaction, occupational lease, option to tax, TOGC, capital allowances, corporate SDLT, Welsh transaction, SDLT or LTT calculation.

## Commercial property process

- Examine heads of terms, commercial contract, property/part plan, CPSEs (licensed where applicable), asbestos/contamination and planning, rights for use/access/services, tenant rent roll and deposits, VAT option evidence, certificates, corporate authority and finance.
- Existing occupational lettings: identify each lease, side letter, rent concession, deposit, break, service charges, arrears, repairs, security of tenure, rent review, guarantees and licences. Avoid assuming the sale is with vacant possession.
- New commercial lease: audit Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 protection or valid contracting out, rent/review, break conditions and notice service, demise, insurance, repairing/dilapidations, alienation, AGA, reinstatement and registration. Formal steps for excluding security of tenure must be independently verified; do not create presumed declarations.
- Lease assignment/surrender: landlord consent, transfer covenants and AGA, releases, VAT on consideration, leasehold registrations/notices, rent deposit and priority of other security.

## VAT and option to tax

Use current HMRC Notice 700/9 and 742A, the actual VAT option/evidence, purchaser VAT details and transaction facts. A TOGC is **not elective**: when legal conditions apply, the TOGC treatment is mandatory. Identify whether the relevant business is to be continued, whether buildings/property are subject to a valid option to tax or other compulsory VAT charging treatment, whether purchaser option/notifications have been made **by relevant time**, and whether anti-avoidance provisions disapply options. Contract drafting cannot dictate tax result.

**Scenario:** seller wants 20% VAT; buyer cannot register for VAT; accountant wants to retain TOGC. Do NOT tell the buyer to retain/remove clause as a substitute for analysis. Obtain documentary option, investigate whether a real property rental business/tenant transfer or another business is being transferred, whether buyer is or must be taxable person, and whether TOGC conditions can be satisfied. Separately assess contractual obligation to pay VAT, SDLT/LTT chargeable consideration, inability to recover input VAT, cashflow and rescission/indemnity provisions. Escalate to VAT specialist where status material or disputed.

## SDLT / LTT structure

**England:** SDLT administered by HMRC. **Wales:** LTT administered by WRA. Rates, reliefs, corporate flat higher rates, non-resident surcharge, multiple dwellings, lease premium/rent NPV, linked transactions, substantial performance, mixed-use, partnerships, exchanges, additional dwelling and charitable/group relief require specialist paths rather than the core calculator. Verify at use point, not just initial date.

The simple calculator in this project implements only dated, verified **ordinary straightforward residential purchase** bands with manually explicit additional-property flag. It refuses use beyond its review date or for complex facts. The rate registry contains official URL, effective date, check date and jurisdiction. An estimate is not a tax return or professional computation. For VAT registered/unregistered parties, corporate purchases, leasehold rental payments, non-UK resident, reliefs, trusts and mixed land, stop and verify the applicable provisions separately.

- HMRC: for notifiable transaction SDLT return and payment usually **within 14 days** of effective date. If payment deadline is weekend/bank holiday follow HMRC instruction to pay by prior working day; verify precise filing/payment rules and effective date.
- WRA: notifiable LTT returns within **30 calendar days beginning after effective date** (LTTA/6010, LTT legislation s44); confirm payment arrangements and exceptions.
- Companies House: MR01 charge registration generally within **21 days starting day after creation** with correct form and certified instrument, subject to relevant law/circumstances. This is distinct from Land Registry registration and contractual lender priorities.

## Completion accounting

Separate completion price, deposit credit, net lender drawdown, cleared client funds, SDLT/LTT, professional charges and VAT, fixed fees, lender retention, apportioned rents/service charges, redemption, seller reimbursements and other disbursements. Identify payer/payee, contractual authority, exact VAT basis, and whether money is actually cleared. Use Decimal arithmetic and a solicitor's double-entry reconciliation. A spreadsheet or automated computation cannot verify bank receipt.
