# Rejected or late company charge registration

Use alongside REGISTRATION.md for supervised investigation and drafting. Do not infer that a submission receipt proves valid delivery or accepted registration.

## Immediate review

1. Record the exact corporate name, number, creation date under the instrument, lender, security and title. Compare the executed instrument with the public company record and filing particulars. Treat any mismatch as an issue for legal review, not permission to amend a signed deed.
2. Preserve the original executed document, submission, receipt, rejection, reasons, email routing and chronology. Record any resubmission and acceptance separately. Calculate the original period from the evidenced creation date and check the applicable statutory regime, including historical exceptions.
3. Identify the deadline, current security position, lender reporting obligations, possible prejudice and the supervising solicitor responsible for the remedy. Escalate a missed deadline promptly. A rejection does not establish that the defect can be cured retrospectively.

## Remedy assessment

Under Companies Act 2006 section 859F, consider whether the statutory conditions for extending the delivery period are met and the appropriate applicant and relief. Extension of time and rectification/replacement remedies are distinct. Do not assume an extension order corrects a defective deed or resolves priority or intervening rights.

Prepare a document list for the lawyer: claim/application appropriate to the verified procedure, factual witness evidence with exhibits, draft order, chronology and any required company/lender authority. Check current jurisdiction, venue, procedural rules, service, court fee and filing requirements directly before use. Old LOC009 examples are historical guidance, not proof of current fees or portal categories.

For a requested online filing walkthrough, inspect the actual authorised portal and record the exact visible court, case type, document type and fee selections with the observation date. Distinguish observed choices from proposed choices. Never invent a dropdown label, assert a court will accept a category, or submit a claim during a research-only task. If login is required, explain precisely which screens remain unverified and continue with public procedure research and draft preparation.

## After an order

Diary the period actually granted by the sealed order, check the required Companies House delivery route and supporting documents, obtain acceptance/certificate and reconcile it to the charge and company. Review the separate HMLR application and any requisitions or section 859H note. Receipt, registration at Companies House and registration at HMLR are separate events with separate evidence.
