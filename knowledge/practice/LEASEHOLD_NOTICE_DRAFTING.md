# Leasehold notices, consents and landlord-management correspondence

Triggers: leasehold notice of transfer/assignment, notice of charge, licences to assign/alter, consent request, certificates of compliance, managing-agent address/fee enquiries. **The actual lease and current title restriction control.** These neutral documents are not prescribed forms: read clause and landlord/management requirements first. Keep exact addresses, notice periods, fees, copies and evidence only when supported.

1. Verify freeholder/management company and current authorised notice service address; do not use an old email or change service method by assumption. Read lease clauses, any deed of variation, management pack, transfer restrictions, landlord consent and time limits.
2. **Transfer/charge:** distinguish registration of deed at HMLR from contractual notice to freeholder and consent or certificate. Establish which transfers/charges require landlord notification, wording, certified copy, fees and creditor/proprietor information. Do not make notice effective by merely writing it.
3. **Licence to assign:** check lease alienation terms, any qualified covenant, AGA, guarantee, financial standing and costs; do not promise consent or assert absence of default.
4. **Licence to alter:** check external/structural restrictions, planning/building consents, reinstatement, asbestos, insurance, landlord surveyor costs and licences; a request is not consent.
5. **Certificate of compliance:** quote the registered restriction verbatim and obtain the exact certificate required; wrong signatory/conditions may result in requisition.
6. Confirm receipt, fee, registration and acknowledgement as distinct file stages. Do not upload actual client documents to public plugin repo.

Drafts in `templates/notices/leasehold/` are editable starting points requiring actual instructions. New residential s47/s48 particulars are in `templates/notices/residential/` and not interchangeable with commercial lease notices. For forms of deed never invent counterpart execution or authority.
