# Landlord and Tenant Act 1954: exclusion of security of tenure

**Scope:** England and Wales, **prospective business tenancies** only, sections 24-28 exclusion under section 38A(1) and the Regulatory Reform (Business Tenancies) (England and Wales) Order 2003 (SI 2003/3096). Read actual documents and check that Part II is relevant. These are not section 25 notices, section 26 requests or Schedule 3/4 notices concerning an **agreement to surrender**.

**Authoritative official source, examined 24 November 2026:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/3096/pdfs/uksi_20033096_en.pdf , Schedule 1 (PDF pp 15-16) and Schedule 2 (pp 17-20), especially paragraphs 2-8; Landlord and Tenant Act 1954 section 38A as inserted by Article 22 of the Order. Before every use, verify current amendment/consolidation and any changed statutory requirements. The PDF's page 20 contains the prescribed statutory jurat; do not replace with an informal witness statement.

## Trigger and legal checkpoints

| When the facts show | Route/document | Legal gate |
|---|---|---|
| Business lease grant / underlease is expressly to be excluded | Serve Schedule 1 notice | Must occur **before** tenant enters tenancy or earlier contractual commitment |
| Notice served **at least 14 days** before earliest binding event | Schedule 2 **paragraph 7 simple declaration** | Tenant / properly authorised declarant makes declaration before first binding event; copy Schedule 1 notice inside it |
| Notice served **less than 14 days** before first binding event | Schedule 2 **paragraph 8 statutory declaration** | Serve notice and administer declaration **before** first binding event; authorised oath-taker and full jurat; copy Schedule 1 notice inside it |
| Date/receipt of notice or earliest binding date unknown | Do not assume route; draft without asserting steps complete | Obtain date and evidence; retain **pending** status |
| 1954 Act exclusion clause / new lease or agreement for lease | Lease clause with correct path | Schedule 2 paragraph 5: notice/declaration referenced in or endorsed on the instrument creating the tenancy; paragraph 6: contracting-out agreement or reference likewise |
| Surrender of an existing protected tenancy | **Schedule 3 + Schedule 4** | Different statutory process, not these templates |
| Existing lease already entered, or alleged late/backdated notice | STOP/ESCALATE | Cannot cure by merely inserting wording or backdating the declaration |

**14-day rule:** Count actual elapsed days between legally effective service and the **earlier** of the lease entering into and becoming contractually bound to enter into it. Do not rely on the planned completion date alone. If calculating contentious boundary dates or method of service, check service provisions and seek supervising solicitor sign-off. "Statutory declaration" is not automatically required where valid 14-day simple declaration applies.

**Prescribed notice:** The Schedule 1 form contains **To: [tenant name and address]** and **From: [landlord name and address]** followed by the full **IMPORTANT NOTICE**. The notice has **no property, lease date, signature or service-date box** in its prescribed layout. Record service evidence on a separate matter record; do not amend the notice body to add information not prescribed. Match warning text exactly as reproduced in Schedule 2 declarations.

**Declarations:** Use the prescribed alternatives carefully. For an individual declarant who is the tenant, remove unused options and omit "I am duly authorised" if not applicable; for a company or representative, identify the tenant and the declarant's valid authority. All particulars must come from client/source documents. A simple declaration is made and dated by the appropriate person, with signature evidence. A statutory declaration is solemnly declared before an oath-taking professional, with jurat, place, actual declaration date, signature and authorised recipient's details. The blank document or a draft email proves nothing. No automatic oath or service.

**Execution wording:** The statutory declaration template reproduces the Schedule 2 paragraph 8 text as printed in the 2003 Order, including its formal jurat; do not substitute a generic statement of truth. The statutory instrument uses the words "Statutory Declaration Act 1835" (singular) in its printed model jurat; the underlying Act is generally cited as the Statutory Declarations Act 1835. Review the chosen wording with the administering solicitor, and do not amend the prescribed text casually.

**Lease:** Use `templates/lta1954/LEASE_CONTRACTING_OUT_CLAUSE.md` only after the procedural route is evidenced. A reference in the lease cannot replace the mandatory notice or declaration. Cross-check the notice, declaration and lease for the same tenant and proposed landlord(s), premises, contractual commitment date and transaction. Keep copies of the served notice, proof of service, dated signed declaration, and final executed lease on the private file.

**Authority and reporting:** See `core/OPERATING_STANDARD.md` and `core/EVIDENCE_AND_RISK.md`; this skill cannot serve, administer an oath, sign, exchange, complete, certify or register. Report source status, missing facts and solicitor approval separately. No fictitious service dates, licence, witness or signed declarations.
