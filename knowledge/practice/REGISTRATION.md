# HM Land Registry, Land Charges and corporate security filings

**Load when:** TR1, TP1, AP1, FR1, DL, CH1, DS1, DS2, K15, OS1/OS2, restrictions, prescribed forms, requisitions and charge registration.

## Form control

Current official HMLR index is `https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/hm-land-registry-forms`. All form names and listed update dates in `knowledge/forms/catalogue.json` were checked against publisher index on 27 October 2026; that is NOT independent binary form revision verification. Retrieve the latest original Word/PDF and matching instructions when actually preparing a form. Check Land Registration Rules 2003 and HMLR PG8 (updated 14 September 2026) for prescribed execution.

Use official original form; do not rebuild an imitation and submit it as prescribed. Fill only verified fields and retain every instruction, annotation, prescribed clause and panel. Record fields changed and supporting evidence. If user supplies a PDF only, an accessible editable derivative should be labelled working copy and visually/digitally verified against original before signature/submission.

## Common workflows

- Transfer of whole registered estate: TR1, signed as deed where applicable, date on completion, tax compliance proof where needed and AP1. TR1 panel 7 must not be changed, including in templates.
- Transfer of part: TP1, sufficient plan, granted/reserved easements, retained land risks and possible discharge/release of part; AP1 and certified copies.
- First registration: FR1 + DL in duplicate, title documents/plan, class of title, registration fee and ancillary applications. Refer PG1; originals needed for first registration subject to updated guidance.
- Mortgage discharge: confirm actual e-DS1/DS1/DS3, cancellation and registration acceptability; check all-moneys and further charges, not just redeemed amount. Use PG31 (updated June 2026).
- Priority: OS1 whole, OS2 part, obtain official search result and calculate actual 30 **working day** expiry from relevant receipt/day-list rules; do not equate local spreadsheet date to statutory cut-off without details.
- Restrictions/notices: read every operative register entry and PG19; choose RX1/RX3/RX4/RXC etc only for the specific legal event. Consent by beneficiary is distinct from cancellation or withdrawal.
- Unregistered Land Charges: K15 full search and K16 bankruptcy-only as appropriate. Verify names, search periods and results. Search only from 1855 per April 2026 K15 guidance.

## Requisitions

For each HMLR requisition record application ID, reference/dated request, deadline, exact question, evidence, reply prepared, whether an extension requested/approved, and final acceptance. Never say registration completed because the application was lodged or an acknowledgement received. Escalate cancellation risk to supervising solicitor and lender.

## Companies House

For company-created registrable charge identify creation date under actual instrument, correct company and charge, MR01, certified redacted instrument and fee. Check statutory 21-day clock, error/late filing remedies and actual accepted certificate; separately perfect Land Registry security. Companies House registration alone does not establish correct legal property charge registration.
