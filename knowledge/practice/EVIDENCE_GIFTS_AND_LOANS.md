# Declarations, gifts and loan documents: review workflow

Use for statements of truth, statutory declarations, gifts of funds or land, family loans and bridging finance. This is a research and review aid; it is not an executed instrument, legal opinion or a substitute for current primary sources and lender-specific requirements.

## Evidence statements and declarations

Identify the recipient and purpose, relevant application and acceptable prescribed form, declarant's identity/capacity, firsthand knowledge and chronology, source documents and exhibits, uncertainty, contradictions and missing facts. Keep direct knowledge separate from information supplied by another person. Verify the applicable wording, signing, witnessing or swearing requirements and any special accessibility requirements with current official guidance for the recipient. Never invent a jurat, declaration, signature, attestation or evidence. For HM Land Registry applications consult [practice guide 73](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statements-of-truth/practice-guide-73-statements-of-truth); its guidance concerns land registration and should not be assumed to apply to every court or other document.

## Gifts of funds or land

Distinguish an outright gift, repayable loan, conditional arrangement and transfer of an interest in land. Record donor and recipient, relationship, amount or property/title, source-of-funds evidence, occupation/beneficial interest assertions, conditions, repayment expectations, insolvency and tax issues, independent advice requirements and actual lender disclosure/approval. Do not infer gift status or fabricate a gift letter or deed from missing instructions. For land transfers review title, consideration, charge/security, consent, execution and registration; use current official forms and [HMLR practice guide 8](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/execution-of-deeds/practice-guide-8-execution-of-deeds) and seek specialist tax advice where appropriate.

## Bridging and other loans

Read the actual offer, facility agreement, charge, guarantees, security documents, valuation assumptions and all special conditions. Produce a clause-linked table for borrower/lender/guarantor, amount, fees, retained interest, interest and default interest, advance conditions, drawdown and repayment dates, exit strategy, extensions, restrictions, additional security, priorities, undertakings, enforcement triggers, redemption and required consents/independent advice. Record ambiguities and inconsistent terms as enquiries; do not calculate an assumed payoff or certify an individual lender's conditions. Reconcile the lender's written instructions with security and completion evidence.

Use `templates/EVIDENCE_GIFT_LOAN_REVIEW.md` to document the work. Send any proposed document for human legal, tax and lender-specific review before execution, filing or reliance.
