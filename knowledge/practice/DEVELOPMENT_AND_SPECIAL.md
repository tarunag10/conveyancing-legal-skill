# New-build, corporate, cross-border, probate, trusts and specialist property

**Load when:** new-build developer, transfers of part, infrastructure easements, probate, trust, shared ownership, Help to Buy, overseas entities, rentcharge, development security, enfranchisement or specialist finance.

## New-build and development

Review approved plans vs contract plans, plot measurements, adoption agreements for roads and sewers, rights to utilities, easements and management company rights, street works, s106/CIL, reserved matters and planning conditions, completion/warranty certification, snagging/retention terms, developer insolvency/deposit protection, reservation fee and notice to complete. Confirm lender incentives disclosure and valuation assumptions. A highway drawing is not an adoption certificate.

## Transfers of part and estate schemes

For TP1 inspect marked plan, exact grant/reservation of rights, benefit/burden of estate rentcharge covenants, fencing/boundary obligations, supply and service media, overage/clawback and lender release of part. Check that both retained and sold land are legally functional.

## Corporate parties and overseas entities

For UK companies confirm actual constitution, director authority, s44 Companies Act 2006 compliant execution, s46 delivery of deeds, Companies House filings where applicable, shareholder benefit and solvency/undervalue risk. For overseas entities consider Register of Overseas Entities and HMLR PG78 (updated September 2026), overseas company registration and dissolution, legal execution authority and applicable restrictions before registration.

## Probate and trusts

Identify registered owner, proof of death, grant, personal representatives' sale/assent power, survivorship and overreaching, Form A restrictions and correct AS1/TR1 route. A death certificate alone may not prove a representative's authority. For trust-owned property inspect instrument, appointments, beneficial shares, required trustee consents and evidence that capital money can be properly received.

## Shared ownership and housing support

Verify the actual scheme lease and retained equity percentage, occupancy, rent and review, staircasing, resale nomination, lender restrictions, provider consent, restrictions on title and original SDLT/LTT election. Do not treat legacy Help to Buy or a closed new-applicant scheme as inapplicable to a still-running equity loan; check the actual loan terms and administrator redemption process.

## Enfranchisement and lease extension

Determine whether route is statutory or voluntary, legal eligibility and prescribed notice, title/deed variations, valuation, funding and leasehold registration, distinguishing reforms actually in force from planned 2024 Act provisions. Refer a contested valuation or statutory timetable to specialist counsel/valuer as appropriate.
