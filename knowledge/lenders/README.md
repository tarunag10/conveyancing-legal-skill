# England & Wales: lender Certificate of Title / Report on Title routing

**Status:** Solicitor-supervised research aid; reviewed against identified official public sources 20 November 2026. This is not a lender instruction, lawyer certification, or authority to send a CoT, give an undertaking, request/release funds, or mark any security condition satisfied. For the detailed record see `lender-directory.json`. **Not exhaustive of all lenders or products.**

## Use on every lender-funded matter

1. **Identify the exact lender, trading name, mortgage account/reference, legal borrower(s), charged property/properties, purpose and lender representation.** Check offer, instruction letter, facility/loan documents, title numbers and completion date. A trading-group connection never makes its member's CoT interchangeable (for example Rely vs Kent Reliance, Barclays retail vs commercial, HSBC UK vs HSBC Private Bank, NatWest residential vs NatWest commercial). Multiple charged properties, borrower company, existing first charges, second charges and third-party security must be described and cleared separately.
2. **Determine the document being requested:** (a) standard Law Society/UK Finance-approved Certificate of Title, (b) short CoT, (c) lender-branded CoT/firm-specific static PDF/Word, (d) case-specific electronic CoT (eCoT), (e) separate-representation solicitor **Report on Title**, (f) lender-specialist RoT and drawdown conditions. Never treat a draft report, Lender Exchange portal membership, mortgage offer, legal charge or TR1 as the equivalent of a valid CoT. A TR1 transfers land; the CoT/Report on Title is a separate lender reporting/funding instruction.
3. **Read the authoritative applicable instruction in order:** (i) matter's current lender letter and offer special conditions; (ii) for participating residential lenders, current England/Wales UK Finance Handbook Part 1, **that lender's Part 2 (especially §4.5b, §10.2b, §1.11)** and, if instructed and applicable, Part 3 lender-only instructions; (iii) lender's solicitor/completions guidance, panel instructions, mortgage deed/terms and any separate-representation requirements; (iv) current case-specific portal communications. Handbook participation is voluntary. Bridging, buy-to-let companies and commercial facilities may rely on separate bespoke instructions. The new UK Finance portal has operated since 29 June 2026; login/registration may be needed: <https://lendershandbook.ukfinance.org.uk/>.
4. **Find the actual document:** inspect the lender's own lawyers page, current mortgage-offer enclosures, or **LMS Lender Documents** <https://www.lms.com/conveyancerzone/pages/documents.aspx>. When eCoT is required, log into <https://www.lms.com/conveyancerzone/account> and locate the **case**, not merely public static downloads. LMS explicitly states its lender documents can change and must not be saved for reuse. Law Society's approved/short forms are at <https://www.lawsociety.org.uk/topics/property/approved-certificate-of-title>. Use them only if lender authorises; do not reproduce or alter protected lender/Law Society wording without licence/authority.
5. **Verify the accepted delivery method** in the particular offer: LMS eCoT, Lender Exchange, Smoove, lender-specific digital system, lender email, postal original, or the lender's own instructed solicitor. **Panel membership does not mean that CoT submission is through that panel's portal.** Secure Link for queries and an LMS document download do not automatically establish eCoT routing. Do not send a signed certificate to an unverified address. Never attach client files to an insecure/unconfirmed recipient.
6. **Read timing carefully.** Capture **working/business/calendar days**, start and cut-off time, advance bank transfer method, fee, notice effective only when **accepted or validated**, weekends/bank holidays, availability of urgent escalation, mortgage-offer expiry, and required lender decision on unresolved reports. The directory gives no universal timing rule. Record the actual target completion date and calculate the latest submission deadline after checking lender's live timetable.
7. **Before solicitor certifies:** review legal title/lease and plan, registered proprietors/beneficiaries, charge ranking, first-charge redemption or second-charge consent/deed of priority, additional security, searches, indemnity approval, buildings insurance, occupation/tenancy, building safety, company borrower powers, AML/SOF, UK Finance disclosure-of-incentives where applicable, signed lender documents, PG/ILA, priority/OS1/OS2 and bankruptcy searches, completion funds and lender conditions. If a concern is material, **report and obtain written lender approval** before an unqualified CoT. Never pre-sign or backdate. A lender-specific CoT can incorporate requests for release of the mortgage advance and certify that legal conditions are met.
8. **Submission and receipt are distinct.** The human authorised conveyancer reviews/signs/submits, captures the portal submission reference or lender acknowledgement, follows up where no receipt, logs outcome and funds timing, and handles re-submission/revision/cancellation exactly as lender requires. Recheck if completion date, loan amount, borrower, lender, title or property changes. A sent CoT without acceptance is not a confirmed drawdown.
9. **Completion remains blocked** until all required lender decisions, executed instruments, valid completion monies and compliance evidence are in the firm's approved file and a qualified human sign-off occurs. This skill must never sign or transmit legal certifications, make lender representations, submit forms, or release funds on its own.

## Interpreting the directory

`lender-directory.json` has 117 discoverable lender/brand entries in this snapshot. Status indicators matter:

- `verified_submission`: primary lender / UK Finance guidance confirms at least one specified submission route within the stated scope. **Recheck live offer.**
- `verified_conditional`: publicly verified **only when a specified scenario is present** (e.g. LMS-issued case, separate representation, brand/product, or a secondary update needs further check).
- `verified_document_only`: official lender or LMS document location confirmed; **submission route NOT verified**.
- `verified_directory_or_panel`: known panel/portal listing, no CoT route confirmed.
- `lookup_only`: a relevant lender name and the generic research route, but no independently confirmed lender-specific form/submission instructions.

Every entry includes the full direct source URL(s), research date, notes, contact only where evidenced, and an optional **source-defined** notice period. The presence of an entry is not a representation that this lender currently lends on that product or is on the user's firm's panel. **Do not infer an unknown channel, contact, notice or CoT availability.** The directory is only a snapshot and must be updated from official current sources.

## Case scenario patterns (the differences that matter)

### Rely Mortgages

Use Rely's official [conveyancing page](https://www.relymortgages.co.uk/intermediaries/conveyancing) → official [LMS lender documents](https://www.lms.com/conveyancerzone/pages/documents.aspx) → the firm's live LMS case and eCoT where available. The public LMS list shows Rely's BTL solicitor instructions, mortgage standard terms, letting criteria and mortgage deed, but **not a public standalone CoT**. For an inaccessible case/eCoT contact Rely Underwriting Case Updates **03300 581697**, then LMS panel assistance **0343 221 0643 / lenderpanels@lms.com** or LMS technical support **0343 221 0610 / support@lms.com**. Never substitute the Kent Reliance CoT.

### Together

Determine representation and lender's lawyer role. Together's published packaging guide describes a **separate lender solicitor** preparing the lender's **Report on Title**, based on documents the borrower's solicitor sends, before Together releases funds. This is not a single borrower-side eCoT process. Confirm current Together matter instructions, deadline, funding mechanism and lender solicitor document checklist. No automatic report sent or drawdown may be assumed. Source: <https://togethermoney.com/-/media/files/cf-packaging-guide.pdf> (historical guide, reverify; not binding for every product).

### LMS eCoT users

**HSBC UK:** mandatory LMS eCoT, six working days' notice, official conveyancer help line **0370 600 7722**. **Bank of Ireland UK:** LMS-managed offers use case management eCoT only; non-LMS case directions are on offer front page. **Skipton:** LMS eCoT, seven business days, amendment number **0345 607 9848**. **Atom:** LMS eCoT only, three working days, borrower must have accepted latest offer in Atom App and outstanding queries closed. **Clydesdale:** LMS if originally instructed by LMS, otherwise Clydesdale non-LMS team per Part 2. These are distinct case rules, not a universal LMS rule.

### Lender Exchange / distinct methods

**Santander UK residential** and **Barclays Bank UK residential** specify Lender Exchange in England/Wales Part 2 §4.5b. **Halifax** currently provides Lender Exchange or specified postal address in publicly indexed Part 2; do not import the separate Lloyds rule. **Lloyds** has a reported 3 November 2026 Part 2 amendment to Lender Exchange only; this is from a *secondary update tracker*, so check current primary Part 2 before acting. **Metro Bank** Part 2 specifies `mortgagecompletions@metrobank.plc.uk` and at least **five working days**, with shorter periods rejected. **Virgin Money** specifies `cots@virginmoney.com`. **Kensington** publishes `completions@kensingtonmortgages.co.uk`, with case reference in subject. **Principality** confirms its **Smoove** portal and on-screen submitted status. **Kent Reliance** publishes its own PDF CoT specifying `Completions@krbs.com` and at least five working days.

**Barclays Business/Commercial** is a separate workflow: the business solicitor instruction page says whether to use a CoT or RoT; for residential property subject to AST/lease it calls for RoT, whereas CoT is for owner-occupied residential. It mentions signed paper submission no fewer than seven days before completion, with instructed online RoT/Collateral Portal alternatives. Do not apply this business page to Barclays Bank UK residential CoTs without instructions.

**Nationwide** publishes CoT M601 via its lawyers forms page. The bank's completion page specifies at least **five working days for BACS** or **48 hours for specifically requested CHAPS** (fee may apply). It does not establish Lender Exchange as the universal CoT submission route.

### Static documents in LMS

The LMS public list contains lender CoT forms for Bath, BHO, Bluestone, Buckinghamshire, Chorley, Cynergy, Danske, Darlington, Dudley, Harpenden, Hinckley & Rugby, Investec, Leeds (manual form), LiveMore, Marsden, Melton (self-build variant), Molo/ColCap (verify currently listed), Monmouthshire (special MBS-prefix variant), Newcastle, Family BS, The Mortgage Lender (BTL versus residential), Vernon and others. Each form is **only a source location** until the case-specific submission method is established. Check correct lender legal entity, offer date and product.

## Source change control

For any new case, retrieve current versions, record the **time and actual source** checked, and ensure a solicitor reviews changed CoT text. A URL accessible in November 2026 does not establish future applicability. Do not use web search summaries as substitutes for the primary page/document or the matter's offer. Do not crawl authenticated LMS, Lender Exchange, Smoove or bank portals without authorisation. Change-detection evidence is not legal sign-off. Record uncertain results in `templates/LENDER_COT_ROUTING_AND_SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md` and `templates/SOURCE_REVIEW_RECORD.md`; never mark unknown channels or conditions satisfied.
