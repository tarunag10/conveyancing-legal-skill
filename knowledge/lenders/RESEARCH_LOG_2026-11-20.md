## Evidence gaps and explicit exclusions

- `lender-directory.json` is a **117-entry routing lookup**, not a complete audited census or proof of an active current panel for every name. **57 entries are only name-based lookups**, and the others include documented routes, document-only and panel-only categories.
- The lender names on UK Finance Reach Out include lenders and product brands. Their presence says nothing about whether they currently write new mortgages or accept the generic Law Society CoT.
- LMS directory is a **form/source inventory**, not a public record of whether its electronic CoT service is compulsory for every lender. We categorically do not convert an LMS listed CoT into `lms_ecot` without a separate lender-specific instruction.
- The UK Finance new portal can require sign-in. A current official Part 2 may change after this research; no automated scraping or account bypass was performed.
- The Lloyds 3 November 2026 reported change is expressly **secondary** and must be confirmed in the present official UK Finance Part 2 before instructing a solicitor to use Lender Exchange only.
- Some PDF source content was available only as the published PDF's indexed text where full screenshot verification was unavailable. These cases are individually marked as dated snapshots, not a full certification of the file/version.
- No user-specific case records, bank details, identity or passwords are included. This directory cannot itself submit a CoT or RoT, obtain a lender receipt, or draw down mortgage funds.
