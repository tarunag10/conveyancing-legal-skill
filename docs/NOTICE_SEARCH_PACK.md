# Notice and searches extension pack, 24 November 2026

## What is new

Five specialised, independently routed skills (commercial business tenancies; property searches; leasehold notices; commercial enforcement; England private residential notices); **17 official-form preparation worksheets** for 1954 Act Part II Forms 1–17 with legal triggers and links; three 2003 Order Schedules 3–4 surrender-related neutral Word notices/declarations; **14 search request/result review sheets** with official source routing; **six leasehold correspondence precedents**; **eight enforcement and LTA 1954 tenant notices/agreements**; **four residential preparation/templates**, plus service and deadline logs. Some serviceable instruments require completion under licensed or currently prescribed government originals and are explicitly not bundled as imitation DOCX statutory forms.

## Legal design

- For forms prescribed by legislation, check exact statutory form and current government version at point of use, and keep official wording/notes. Worksheets are internal only and cannot be issued as notices.
- For non-prescribed contractual notices, user-supplied executed document, current title and actual recipient/method/time are needed; no fake names, dates, execution or service.
- For section 146 forfeiture or Form 3A possession, do not auto-issue a serviceable notice; supervising solicitor must select correct version, grounds and preconditions.
- Under 2003 Order, warning notice/declaration for **new business grant** (Schedules 1–2) differs from prospective **agreement to surrender** (Schedules 3–4). Actual signed surrender deed remains separate.
- England private sector changes from 1 May 2026 are separate from Wales occupation contracts and social housing; form version and transition must be checked.
- Local Land Charges official search moves to HMLR by local authority area; CON29 and CON29O continue with relevant local authorities; no unlicensed Law Society reproduction.

## Validation

Source verification and form selection are ongoing; this is not an automated licence, approval, search-submission, sworn declaration or service engine. Word documents have neutral blanks and require render/content review before release to another party. Canonical forms and user matter sources control over any packaged illustrative wording. Existing skill modules, assets and metadata remain intact.
