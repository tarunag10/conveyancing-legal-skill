# Notice and Search Pack, v0.4.0 (24 November 2026)

- Preserved existing v0.3.0 commercial lease masters, contracting-out templates, lender instructions and all earlier workflows.
- Added five skills for protected business tenancy notices, conveyancing searches, leasehold notices, enforcement and residential notice routing.
- Added 17 official-form preparation worksheets for SI 2004/1005 Forms 1–17, with official source references and clear NOT FOR SERVICE labels.
- Added 2003 Order Schedule 3 surrender warning and Schedule 4 simple/statutory declaration editable blank templates, separate from new tenancy contracting-out forms.
- Added OS1, OS2, OS3, SIM, OC1, OC2, K15, K16, LLC1, CON29, CON29O, environmental, drainage and highway request/result review sheets.
- Added six leasehold notice/consent models, eight commercial enforcement and 1954 Act tenant notice/extension models, residential post-May-2026 official-form worksheets and section 47/48 LTA 1987 aids.
- Introduced evidence and deadline logs, official-form vs worksheet/source licensing safeguards, and additional routing in both skill launcher and workflow router.
- All notices are blank; neither the existence of a template nor creation of a letter effects service, execution, registration, search or legal approval.
